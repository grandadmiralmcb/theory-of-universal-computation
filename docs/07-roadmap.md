# 07 — Roadmap, Open Questions & Contentions

## Current Contentions (live tensions)

1. **Monism vs. formal entailment**  
   Informational monism is the unique reading that avoids idle structure, regress, or unexplained contact. It is not yet a theorem. Contention: can the dynamics be tightened until non-informational alternatives become incoherent rather than merely costly?

2. **Multi-path quantitative behavior**  
   Sharing supplies the substrate for simultaneous dependence on multiple residuals. Quantitative weights (interference visibility, phase structure) are not yet recovered from structural disruption alone. This is the largest remaining formal soft surface.

3. **Amplitude structure**  
   Is a complex amplitude structure forced, derivable from sharing + disruption, or must it be re-introduced as a controlled enrichment?

4. **Hard problem of consciousness**  
   The theory accounts for the structure of coherent experience but explicitly does not claim to have explained why high-\(\kappa\) sequential evaluation is felt.

5. **Empirical contact**  
   The structural continuum limit and the decoherence inequality (coherence lost when expected future disruption cost of preserving shares exceeds cost of breaking them) are still high-level. Concrete numerical predictions remain thin.

## Open Questions (updated)

### Immediate technical
1. Can a precise function of cumulative and expected \(S\) assign structural path weights that reproduce basic interference visibility curves without primitive amplitudes?
2. Write the continuum argument for \(m_{\rm struct}\) and \(b_{\rm struct}\) in fully explicit discrete-to-continuum form suitable for simulation.
3. What is the minimal discrete-tick simulation that can test the structural Newtonian regime and the structural decoherence criterion?

### Structural / emergent
4. How (if at all) do gauge-like symmetries and particle spectra arise from sharing architecture and disruption minimization alone?
5. Can a finite effective sequential signal speed be forced from low-disruption preference + sharing, and can it be made consistent with Lorentz invariance?
6. How do multi-particle and continuum-field regimes emerge as large-scale limits of expression clusters?

### Foundational
7. Is there a route from the current dynamics to strict entailment of informational monism, or is the “unique non-idle reading” the strongest achievable status?
8. What is the precise dynamical status of non-temporal fixed points under purely structural disruption preference?
9. How much of observed physics can be shown to emerge from the reduced basis versus how much still requires controlled enrichment?

### Meta-theoretic / empirical
10. What would constitute a decisive empirical failure of the structural disruption preference itself?
11. Are the six reduced primitives still minimal, or can the generative core be reduced further without loss of sequential projection and structural cost?

## Priority order for further dogfooding
1. Structural path weights / interference from sharing + disruption (close the largest formal gap).
2. Explicit discrete continuum construction + minimal simulator.
3. Test whether monism can be raised from “unique non-idle reading” to something closer to entailment.
4. Only then re-examine gauge structure, spectra, and spacetime geometry.
