# 00 — Theory Charter (corrected foundation)

This document overrides earlier overclaims where they conflict. Finite expression trees remain the default *working model*; they are not the ontology.

---

## 1. What the theory is about

**Name (working):** Expression-Tree Ontology / Structural Sequential Calculus  
**Actual subject:** Structured, evaluable information with *locally constructed sequential order*, dynamics that prefer low structural disruption, and irreversible projection when multi-path co-dependence becomes expensive.

Origin thread: temporal nonlinearity, programmable substrate, phenomenological coherence, non-temporal attractors, consciousness as sequential linearizer — refined into a cost-and-selection dynamics on shareable informational structure.

---

## 2. Ontological commitments (thin and fixed)

### Exists
1. **Structured evaluable information** — patterns that can be composed, shared, and reduced under strategies.
2. **Local sequentialization** — sequential order is not a global background parameter; it is constructed by evaluators through successive reduction.
3. **Structural disruption** — changes to sharing, binding, and observational identity have comparable cost; dynamics prefer lower cost.
4. **Unprojected structure remains real** — alternatives not currently on a high-coherence sequential trajectory are not unreal; they are not selected.

### Preferred reading (not theorem)
**Informational monism:** the informational structure *is* the substrate. Non-informational duplicates are idle, regressive, or unexplained in contact. This is the unique non-idle reading, not a formal entailment.

### Not claimed as ontology
- Finite expression trees / graphs as the only allowed form of structure.
- Discrete ticks as fundamental time.
- Complex amplitudes as generated from real disruption counts alone.
- Continuum spacetime as fundamental (nor as forbidden — it is emergent or idealized).

## 3. Working model vs ontology

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Ontology** | Structured evaluable information; local sequentialization; preferential low disruption; real unprojected residuals |
| **Working model** | Finite terms (`app`, `abs`, `pair`, `eq`, `reduce`, `share`); integer counters \(S,B,D\); discrete ticks |
| **Continuum idealization** | Sequential parameter \(x\in\mathbb{R}\), \(\tau\to 0\), ODEs, smooth potentials |
| **Quantum hosting layer** | Linear residuals with share weights \(a\in\mathbb{C}\); free-epoch unitary dynamics under extended cost / regularity; structural projection as non-unitary locus |

**Rule:** Theorems proved in the working model are exact there. Continuum and QM results are limits or hosted structure unless separately derived.

Finite trees were a thought-exercise representation of composable information under temporal nonlinearity. They stay as the lab bench (exact T6, executable decoherence, sims). They are not a cage around the ontology.

## 4. Dynamics (corrected)

### Core dynamical principle
**Preferential low-disruption sequentialization:** among admissible reductions, select minimal structural disruption.

Disruption is any change that breaks co-dependence, opens bindings, or alters observational identity. In the working model this is \(C=\alpha S+\beta B+\gamma D\). In a more general model it may be a real-valued or measure-theoretic disruption functional on information structure — same principle, broader carrier.

### Classical sequential calculus (working model + continuum idealization)
- Inertia from sharing density (share count in the finite model).
- Bias from contextual excess disruption.
- Continuum limit under smooth bias: \(\ddot x = -b/m_{\rm struct}\).
- Sharp prediction (finite model): inverse share-count acceleration ratio (T6), executable end-to-end.

### Multi-path and projection
- Co-dependence via sharing.
- Isolation cost vs maintain cost; when isolation is cheaper, paths drop (**structural decoherence**).
- Classical limit of coherence = singleton under preferential sequentialization.

### Quantum sector (hosted, constrained — not fully derived)
- **A4:** residual algebra may be linear with complex share weights (type upgrade of the working model when multi-path interference is required).
- **Free epoch:** co-dependence still cheap to maintain.
- **Extended cost D19** (when weights present): support collapse and relative-modulus change are charged → free epochs freeze support and relative moduli.
- **B_flow:** free-epoch weight maps are invertible flows (regularity).
- **Consequence:** free-epoch evolution unitary on active weight space; structural projection is the sole non-unitary locus.
- **Born rule:** reading of \(|a|^2\) at projection — not derived from disruption counts alone.
- **Phase generator \(\varphi_i\):** open (structural proxy allowed as enrichment).

**Honesty line:** The theory *derives* classical sequential limits and structural multi-path coexistence/decoherence from preferential disruption minimization. It *hosts* interference and Born statistics inside a constrained linear weight layer. It does not claim complex amplitudes fall out of integer share-breaking.

## 5. Continuity and probability (corrected stance)

**Continuity.** Not native to the finite working model. Continuum physics is an idealization of refined sequentialization. Weakness if finiteness is ontologized; neutral if finiteness is only the working model. Future work: continuum or measure-theoretic carriers of the same dynamical principle without pretending finite trees are mandatory.

**Probability.** Main gap is measure-from-structure (Born), not discreteness of projection events. Finite outcome sets are enough for two-path toys; continuous spectra need continuum residual families or a continuum carrier.

## 6. What stays, what is demoted

### Stays (core)
- Local sequentialization (temporal nonlinearity as evaluation order).
- Preferential low disruption.
- Sharing / co-dependence and costly isolation.
- Unprojected residuals real.
- Structural inertia from sharing; classical limit under bias.
- T6 as exact theorem + executable test in the finite model.
- Structural decoherence as irreversible projection.
- Informational monism as preferred non-idle reading.

### Demoted
- Finite expression trees as *the* form of reality → working model only.
- R★/N★ as theorems of bare three-counter cost → false; use D19 + B_flow.
- “Recovery of QM” as full derivation → structurally constrained hosting of linear kinematics + Born reading.
- Continuum Newton as native discrete identity → continuum idealization of sequential calculus.

### Abandoned as claims
- Amplitudes derived from \(S,B,D\) alone.
- Unitarity from bare cost alone.
- Monism as formal theorem.
- Consciousness identified exhaustively with high-\(\kappa\) sequentialization.
