## Success of Hilbert-space mathematics

Hilbert space is an exceptionally well-adapted *effective* description of amplitude-valued multi-path reduction (`force_A`) in coherent sequential regimes. Superposition, interference, Born-rule statistics, and entanglement arise naturally from the amplitude structure and shared-subexpression recombination already present in Route A. The SM’s empirical triumph demonstrates the power of that effective linear-amplitude language once correct particle content and gauge structure are supplied. In the present framework Hilbert space is relocated from fundamental ontology to highly successful calculational framework for a particular dynamical regime.

## Hard problems of the SM and corresponding resources here

- **Quantum gravity / status of spacetime** — Spacetime order is not fundamental; sequential evaluation and bounded signal generate causal structure. Background independence is built-in.
- **Hierarchy / naturalness** — Cost minimization dynamically disfavors high-cost fine-tunings.
- **Dark matter** — Additional high-resistance, weakly interacting vacuum excitations are natural.
- **Measurement problem** — Definite outcomes arise from cost landscape when a microscopic excitation binds to a macroscopic sequential evaluator.
- **Origin of gauge group & particle content** — Labels + linear typing + cost landscape constrain allowed spectra and interactions; the observed content can in principle be a lowest-cost consistent solution.
- **Vacuum energy** — Related to residual cost/complexity of the vacuum expression \(V\) itself; sequential evaluators couple only to certain high-\(\kappa\) projections, opening room for dynamical suppression.
- **Matter–antimatter asymmetry** — Complex phases in amplitudes together with possible cost asymmetries between matter-like and antimatter-like excitations.

These remain structural capabilities and reformulations, not yet quantitative solutions.
