# 03 — Quantum / Particle Sector (Route A)

Controlled syntactic enrichment that opens quantitative contact with quantum-like phenomena while remaining inside the expression ontology.

## New / Refined Primitives

1. **Amplitude-valued reduction**  
   \(\texttt{force}_A : \texttt{Expr} \to \mathcal{A}(\texttt{Val})\)  
   where \(\mathcal{A}\) is an amplitude structure (working choice: \(\mathbb{C}\)).  
   Multiple reduction paths receive weights; recombination produces interference.

2. **Linear / resource-sensitive composition**  
   `lcomp`, `lapp` consume their arguments. Ordinary unrestricted copying is restricted by labels.

3. **Labels / charges**  
   Expressions carry additive labels drawn from appropriate groups or representation categories. Linear composition enforces conservation syntactically.

4. **Vacuum expression \(V\)**  
   Distinguished lowest-cost, maximally symmetric background.  
   Particle-like objects = minimal stable, labeled excitations of \(V\).

5. **Phase / orientation** (optional)  
   Extends cost or shared-subexpression links so that path recombination can produce constructive or destructive interference.

## Emergent Particle Behavior

- **Discrete spectra** — cost minimization + vacuum + labels select discrete minimal stable excitations (particle species). Rest-cost plays the role of rest mass.
- **Propagation** — sequential trajectory of an excitation under ambient constraints and bounded signal \(c\).
- **Interference** — amplitudes from distinct paths add upon recombination.
- **Conservation laws** — enforced by typing; forbidden label combinations are ill-formed.
- **Measurement / definite outcomes** — binding of a microscopic excitation to a large high-resistance sequential evaluator makes high-\(\kappa\) definite trajectories strongly preferred (cost-based decoherence).
- **Interactions** — additional stable expression patterns carrying appropriate labels can mediate forces.

## Compatibility with Classical Regime

When an excitation (or bound collection) becomes large, high-resistance, and strongly entangled with a macroscopic environment, multi-path coherence is suppressed by the cost landscape. Surviving dynamics reduce to ordinary lowest-cost sequential trajectories — the classical regime already derived.
